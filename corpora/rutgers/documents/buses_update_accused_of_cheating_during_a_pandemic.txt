THREAD: [UPDATE] Accused of cheating during a pandemic

Here is my original post, please read that before reading this post.

​

On April 9th, 2020 I took my 20 multiple choice accounting midterm exam and was flagged for cheating. I was told to request a University hearing by my fellow peers as their evidence didn't seem plausible. A University Hearing consists of 3 jurors, 2 students and 1 faculty member. Not sure if this matters but during my hearing the 2 students were both in grad school.

​

On August 3rd I had my (virtual) hearing,

I had to first prepare for my hearing, but in order to start preparing for my hearing I need a basis to start off of. The good thing is when you request a University hearing you get to view the University's evidence. They also set a deadline for me to share my evidence with them, my evidence was merely just my phone records as they had alleged that I may have been communicating with other students during my exam. The University shared their evidence with me the night before my University hearing. I found it really unfair that Rutgers can just share their evidence with you whenever they want so long as its before the hearing date, but I the defendant have to share all my evidence before a set deadline? They also blacked out most of the evidence on the 3 page document they provided me and I only found out what it said under the blacked out text during the actual hearing, in other words I had to prepare for a response on the spot during the hearing. You're allowed to bring in a factual and/or character witness with you, I brought in a factual witness, to help dispute the issue regarding the timings. I had a group hearing with two students whom I have never spoken to and we were all asked if we knew each other, we all stated no. I even stated "I don't even know these peoples faces"

The University brought up 4 pieces of evidence and we all responded to it.

​

- the answers were identical

​

The Dean explained to us that we all had the same answers, given that this was a 20 multiple choice exam, I actually didn't believe I had the same answers as the other alleged students with me, so I asked to see my exam, and the Dean said "I don't have it, I was told you guys all had the same right and wrong answers" I responded "Ok, when we had the same wrong answers, did we pick the same wrong choice for each wrong answer, as there are 3 wrong answers in each multiple choice question?" He responded "I don't know", I said "No further questions" I saw this as a victory.

​

- the order the questions were answered is similar

​

The Dean explained that we answered the questions in a similar order and jumped around instead of answering it in the sequential order that it was given in. I responded "I can’t speak for my classmates, however I can share how I go about taking tests. Typically, I answer the questions that I am the most comfortable with first and then go back to rest. Depending on the topics I am the most familiar with, I even pick and choose certain questions to answer first to help get the ball rolling. In doing so, sometimes certain questions offer hints to other questions." Another thing I didn't mention was that all of the questions were shown to you on one page, therefore answering it in a non-sequential order didn't really seem out of the ordinary to me, I even spoke to my fellow classmate and he stated he did the same thing. I can understand the order being suspicious if however the questions weren't all shown on the same page on the other hand.

​

- the time the answers were submitted is identical

The Dean explained to me that we all clicked on each answer within a minute of each-other. In order to help refute this claim I brought in my factual witness. My factual witness is a graduate from Rutgers University with a 4.0 GPA, he majored in Computer Science. He brought up TCP protocols and a lot of other technical terms that I don't really remember and I apologise for that. He mainly went into depth about how this system isn't really reliable, as we are all taking our exams off our respective locations, using different routers, computer specs, service providers, and he explained how human latency could even be taken into account. I remember specifically that the faculty member was impressed with this information as she commended him for his information.

​

- "He said you cheated"

The Dean explained to me that a student made a claim we all cheated and that he in fact cheated with us, he also went onto say that there were about 16 other students cheating in my class during the exam. The Dean explained that he couldn't find those other 16 students that the student claimed were cheating. This same student claimed that I initiated a phone call during the exam, I provided my phone records to prove I didn't do so. I also had to sign a contract stating that I wouldn't harass or confront that specific student, I would assume the other two students with me had to sign it as well.

​

I do want to mention, that this whole hearing was being recorded, but when they were deciding the verdict they asked us all to leave the call and they stated that the recording would now stop. We asked when we would get our results they stated "We're going to decide right now, but you're not going to find out for another 5 business days" I found that really inconsiderate, as we have been waiting 4 months to get this case over with.

​

- The Verdict

Guilty

Why?

- The students order of the questions were almost always identical and the students matched in how they jumped around between the order of questions.

- The students had the same right and wrong answers.

- The Board found that the phone records did not prove that there was no communication between parties as there are other ways in which communication could happen via an app, instant messenger, etc.

- Although the students were named by another party member the board decision was not based on this alone and the board found the other evidence regarding the similarities of the exams to be more influential than this.

What do I think?

- I feel like they completely disregarded my response during the actual hearing and considered this evidence against me.

- I wasn't even shown my exam when I had asked for it, the Dean was even left speechless during the hearing and responded "I don't know" but still used this as evidence to convict me.

- This actually shocked me, the only reason I provided my phone records in the first place was because the University claimed I initiated a phone call, I can go as far as to say that, the student who claimed I initiated a phone call to him doesn't have my phone number, or actually any communication with me on any social platform. I also didn't know any of the students in the hearing with me and they stated they didn't know me either, but we all apparently communicated with one another?

- I don't know why they listed this as a reason, but they listed this as a reason.

What can I do?

I am able to write an appeal to these reasonings if I feel as if the decision made against me was wrong. I was considering doing so, but I feel like I'm going to get the same reasons sent back to me and everything I respond to is going to get disregarded again.

Punishment

- Grade of F in Accounting

- Disciplinary Probation (2 Semesters)
