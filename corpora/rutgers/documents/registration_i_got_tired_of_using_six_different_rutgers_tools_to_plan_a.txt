THREAD: I got tired of using six different Rutgers tools to plan a semester, so I built AtlasRU!!
TOPIC: Schedule

Hey r/rutgers! I’m a student here, and after months of building, testing, and constantly changing things based on feedback, I’m really excited to finally share AtlasRU.

Planning classes usually means jumping between WebReg, CSP, Degree Navigator, Rate My Professors, a course sniper, and screenshots of your friends’ schedules.

AtlasRU brings all of that into one place:

- Professor ratings and course descriptions beside every section

- Filters for courses you’ve completed or actually have the prerequisites for

- Schedule generation, conflict checking, and calendar export

- Graduation tracking and an interactive prerequisite roadmap

- Seat alerts for closed sections

- Friend schedule overlays to find shared free time

- An AI advisor for course and degree questions

Around 20 Rutgers students have been testing it since June, and a lot of what’s there now came directly from their suggestions.

Try AtlasRU: https://atlasru.com

Especially if you’re an incoming freshman, or just someone who wants help figuring out their schedule, give it a try and see what it recommends!!

I also made an Instagram launch post showing everything in action: https://www.instagram.com/p/DbBSCK8AO0W/?igsh=b2x6ZW5lYTJyZjdw

I’m genuinely so excited to finally put this out. Check out the app and the Instagram post, and tell me what you think! Every bug report, confusing screen, missing feature, or random idea helps me make AtlasRU better for Rutgers students!!
