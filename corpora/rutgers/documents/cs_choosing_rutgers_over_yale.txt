THREAD: Choosing Rutgers over Yale
TOPIC: Advice Wanted

For reference, I’m planning on majoring in math or philosophy and then getting a PhD. I know Rutgers has an amazing philosophy department, but I’m concerned about how much attention undergraduates get at Rutgers compared to Yale. Also, of course, the opportunities/connections at Yale are incomparable. It seems I’m now going on a rant so I’ll stop…

edit: Thank you to everyone for the thoughtful responses! Definitely makes me feel better about going to Rutgers :D
