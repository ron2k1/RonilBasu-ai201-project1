THREAD: Got a Harsh Reality Check

I’m a 26 year old non-traditional student who was supposed to graduate this semester with my BA in Math but I failed one of my classes. I’ve always struggled with my mental health and it’s affected school more than not, hence why I’m non-traditional. I was really ready to throw in the towel and not go back to school. I didn’t know how it could get any worse considering I was most likely going to lose the great job offer I worked so hard to get. Even with only one class left, I wanted to quit.

Until I found out on Saturday that my mom has lung cancer. She never smoked a day in her life, never drank, never even ate meat and yet here we are. Despite me taking so long to graduate, she never gave up on me. She always pushed me to study hard and make a life for myself. So even though it seems impossible, I have to graduate this summer. I’m going to take a summer class and do my best. All while working and supporting my mom through her treatment.

For so long, school has been my life alongside work. All the failure I endured in school felt like the end all, be all of my life. Nothing else seemed to outweigh how much of an obstacle it was for me. However, things like this put life into perspective. Failing a class isn’t fun, it sucks. But it’s most definitely not the worst thing in the world. With that being said, it’s time to tackle this summer class and make my mom proud. Wish me luck guys!
