THREAD: The Cheating Here is Ridiculous

As the title says, the cheating here is ridiculous. I’ve had 3 finals these past 3 days (100-200 level courses filled with 100+ people) and the people in the back / middle rows of these giant auditoriums are similar to a group of prompt engineers running on a deadline for the entirety of the exam. Other than in-classroom cheating, the bathroom tactic I’ve noticed has been heavily prevalent too — a student asks to use the restroom, phone jiggling in pocket, and returns 10-15 minutes later to immediately start writing out more content than one can dream.

While I don’t really care what people do directly, I do care when it starts to affect my and the honest peoples grades. Curves are usually based on class averages, and when a large majority of the exam grades are skewed unfairly, it ruins it for the rest of us. I feel like this creates a sort of prisoners dilemma type environment where you’re encouraged to cheat to compete with other cheaters.

The worst part about this is the lack of care from the TAs/professors. In all 3 of my exams, every single one of the ‘proctors’ just sat in the front of the huge room on their laptops.

This needs to stop.
