edit 2: I do not know whether Rutgers will keep online instruction after April 3. However, the CDC has just now recommended that all events with more than 50 people be canceled for the next 8 weeks (i.e., until May 10). That brings us right through the end of finals week. So, at the very least, I very strongly anticipate having to keep online instruction for the remainder of the semester. It's not just international students who would have to manage a time-zone difference. We have students from all across the country! Even a one-hour time difference can make attending an 8:10am class difficult.

There are also students who do not have reliable access to technology. So it's important that all instructors make all course materials available at any time, to the extent possible.

The math department is requiring that all instructors record all lectures. (This can be done in both Canvas and Sakai.) These recorded lectures can then be viewed later. We are currently discussing our policies regarding archiving these recordings permanently and adding captions to the videos.
