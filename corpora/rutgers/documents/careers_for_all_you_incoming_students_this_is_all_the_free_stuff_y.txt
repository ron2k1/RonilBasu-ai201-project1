THREAD: For all you incoming students, this is all the free stuff you could get over 3+ years

Over my time here, I have gathered a ton of free stuff here from various events.

This includes coozies, tote bags, water bottles, notebooks, pens, carabines, electronic stuff, Frances, lanyards, keychains, popsockets, cool packs, towels, pouches, sunglasses, and more cool shit.

Not pictured are also free items Ive gotten and anyone who's been a student can confirm these are handed out: Shirts (Got about 15 Rutgers shirts in different colors), sweatshirt, bucket hat, baseball cap, metal tumbler (the good ones), metal refillable water bottles, insulated shopping bags (courtesy of the career fair), a beach towel, and more.

So don't worry about free swag. You'll have tons of opportunities.
